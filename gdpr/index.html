1. Your Rights Under the EU & UK GDPR (Articles 15–22)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access (Art. 15) & Data Portability (Art. 20): Request confirmation of whether we hold any personal data about you and receive a copy in a structured format.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16) & Erasure / “Right to Be Forgotten” (Art. 17): Request correction or permanent deletion of any personal correspondence you have sent to us.
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18) & Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)): Withdraw cookie or ad personalization consent at any time using the interactive panel above.

2. Google Consent Mode v2 & IAB Europe TCF v2.2

For visitors from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, our advertising implementation respects Google Consent Mode v2 and the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). When personalized ad consent is withheld, Google AdSense serves only non-personalized contextual advertisements.

3. California CCPA / CPRA & U.S. State Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.), Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, and Texas TDPSA, U.S. residents have the right to:

  • Know & Access the categories of personal information collected over the past 12 months.
  • Request Deletion of any personal information submitted via our contact form.
  • Opt Out of “Sale” or “Sharing”: While we never sell personal information for monetary consideration, third-party advertising cookies may be classified as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. Clicking “Reject Optional / Do Not Sell or Share” above disables personalized ad sharing immediately. We also honor browser-level Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.

4. How to Submit a Verified Data Subject Request (DSAR)

To submit a formal GDPR or CCPA request, visit our Contact Us page and select Privacy, GDPR or CCPA Request. We respond to all verified requests free of charge within 15–30 days.
